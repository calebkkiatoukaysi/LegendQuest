## The style (every picture)

STYLE. A pixel-art character portrait for a cosy high-fantasy role-playing game's dialogue box: a chest-up close-up with the face looking straight at the viewer, the head and shoulders square to the camera and centred. Anime-flavoured and charming: large, expressive eyes with a big coloured iris, a dark pupil and one or two bright square highlights; clear, bold eyebrows; a small, simple nose shown with a pixel or two of shadow; a small mouth whose shape changes clearly with the mood; hair in big, clean, chunky locks with a bright highlight band. Real low-resolution pixel art: the whole picture is a grid of about 96 by 96 big square pixels, each about 10 to 11 image pixels wide, every one a single flat colour, with no anti-aliasing, blur, gradient or texture inside it. A small palette of about 24 to 32 colours in ramps of three or four tones per material: shadows cooler and more violet, highlights warmer and more golden. A one-pixel outline in dark brown-violet, never pure black, around the hair, face and shoulders, with lighter coloured lines inside. Soft, colourful cel shading, lit from above and slightly in front. Normal proportions for a bust (not chibi): the age, people and personality of the character obvious at a glance, and the high-fantasy costume shown at the collar and shoulders as bold, simple shapes (trims, clasps, gems, armour edges, fur). Plain, flat background in exactly #00FF00 green, right up to the edges.

AVOID. Smooth, painterly, vector or high-resolution rendering; small or uneven pixels; gradients; photographic texture or noise; glossy 3D-render shading; pure-black outlines. Chibi or super-deformed proportions; a huge head on a tiny body. A three-quarter view, a profile, a tilted head or eyes looking away, unless the expression asks for it. Sweat drops, anger marks, blush lines or tear streams drawn beside the face (the game adds those). Scenery, a frame, a border, a name plate, a speech bubble. Any text, letters, numbers, signature or watermark. Anything copied from an existing game, film, comic or brand.

## The size and framing (every picture)

SIZE. The template (male or female, as the character's entry says) is, exactly 1024 by 1024 pixels. Give it back at exactly 1024 by 1024. Paint the character over the grey mannequin exactly where it is, facing straight out: the eyes on the dashed line across the middle of the head, either side of the dashed centre line; the chin on the dashed line just under the face; the base of the neck on the dotted line below that; the shoulders filling the bottom edge; the top of the hair (or hat, hood or helm) inside the dashed box. The dashed lines are only guides: paint over them, and make the background flat #00FF00 green everywhere else. Don't crop, zoom, re-centre or re-frame it.

## The expressions

- **neutral**: Neutral: calm, eyes open looking straight at the viewer, brows level, mouth closed and relaxed.
- **angry**: Angry: brows pulled hard down and together with a deep crease between them, eyes narrowed and glaring, nostrils flared, teeth gritted, the jaw set.
- **disgust**: Disgusted: eyes narrowed into a flat, unimpressed look, brows lowered and pulled together, nose wrinkled, the upper lip raised on one side in a sneer, the head drawn slightly back.
- **smile**: Warm: eyes softened, cheeks lifted, a gentle closed-mouth smile.
- **laugh**: Laughing: eyes squeezed shut into happy upside-down arcs, brows raised, mouth wide open showing the upper teeth and tongue, cheeks high, the head tipped back a little.
- **sad**: Sad: eyes lowered and glistening with a wet line along the lower lids, the inner ends of the brows raised, the corners of the small mouth turned down, the head dipped a little.
- **grin**: Delighted: brows raised, eyes bright and slightly narrowed with crinkles at the corners, a wide grin showing the upper teeth.
- **smirk**: Knowing: eyes narrowed, one brow raised, one corner of the mouth pulled up.
- **worried**: Worried for someone else: eyes open, brows knit and tilted up in the middle, mouth flat and tight.
- **afraid**: Afraid for yourself: eyes wide with the whites showing, brows tilted up, mouth open, the face pale.
- **cold**: Cold: eyes narrowed and still, brows level, mouth a flat line, no warmth at all.
- **tired**: Tired: eyes half closed with heavy lids, brows level, mouth slack, faint shadows under the eyes.
- **shock**: Shocked: eyes wide, brows raised high, mouth dropped open.
